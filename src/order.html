How to Place Your Order

Follow these simple steps to enjoy your favourite food at Campus Bites.

Ordering Steps

  1. Visit our menu and choose your favourite food.
  2. Place your order at the cafeteria counter.
  3. Confirm your order and make the payment.
  4. Collect your order token and keep it safe.
  5. Wait for your token number to be announced.
  6. Collect your food from the pickup counter.

Payment Methods

  • Cash payment
  • Available digital payment methods
  • Follow the payment instructions at the counter.

Token and Pickup Rules

  • Keep your token until you receive your food.
  • Check your order before leaving the counter.
  • Collect your order when your token is announced.
  • Contact cafeteria staff if there is an issue.

Peak Hours

The cafeteria may be busy during lunch breaks. Place your order early when possible.
